Common questions

My swimmer's events aren't showing

The app links a swimmer to a meet by name, so the first and last name you enter must match how they're registered in the meet program (for example "Thomas," not "Tom"). Check the spelling in Settings → Swimmers, then tap Check for Meet again.

No meet appears when I check

A meet only shows up once someone has imported it and it's the current or upcoming one. If it's early in the week or between meets, there may not be one to load yet.

The lock-screen tile didn't appear

The Live Activity starts automatically around meet time and needs notifications allowed for the app. Make sure notifications are on in iOS Settings → Stingray Swim Meets, and that Live Activities are enabled.

How do I remove my data?

Remove a swimmer in Settings to delete their entries, or delete the app to remove all local data. See the Privacy Policy for details.
