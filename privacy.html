Stingray Swim Meets

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

Stingray Swim Meets is a summer-league swim app for parents to follow their own kids' events, personal-record times, and results. This policy explains what the app collects, where it goes, and how long it's kept. It's published by Ben Rohrs (contact: ben.rohrs@gmail.com).

What the app stores

Information you enter — for each swimmer you add to your roster: first name, last name, gender, and birth year (used only to pick the right events and age-group time standards). A swimmer's "PR" is their seed time from the meet program; the app does not require you to enter one.

Meet information you import — when you import a meet's Entry List and Meet Program PDFs, the app reads the events, heats, and every swimmer's seed time. This parsing happens entirely on your device.

Results — times you enter from the pool-deck scoreboard, either by typing them or by scanning a scoreboard photo.

Live tracking — the current event/heat position, plus a random per-install identifier and an Apple Push Notification (APNs) token, used to show the live "now swimming" status and lock-screen Live Activity across devices.

Where your data goes

On your device (not uploaded): PDF parsing and scoreboard-photo text recognition run entirely on-device using Apple's frameworks. Scoreboard photos are processed locally and are never uploaded.

Our cloud backend (Amazon Web Services, United States): so that one person can import the PDFs and everyone else just sees their own kids, an imported meet — the events and the full roster of swimmer names and seed times taken from the publicly posted meet program — is uploaded to a shared store. Other people using the app can download that meet to see their own swimmers in it. The live event/heat position and your device's push token are also sent so the live tracker and lock-screen updates work. Apple's Push Notification service delivers the lock-screen updates.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use any third-party analytics or advertising/tracking SDKs.

Children's information

The app is intended for parents and guardians, not for use by children. It stores swimmers' names and times, which are often those of minors. That information comes from publicly posted meet programs and pool-deck scoreboards. We collect only what's needed to show your swimmers' events and results, and we don't knowingly allow children to use the app directly or collect information beyond what a meet program already makes public.

How long it's kept

  • Shared meets in the cloud auto-expire about 30 days after they're published.
  • Live event/heat data and device push tokens auto-expire about 48 hours after a meet.
  • Everything else (your roster, imported meets, results) stays on your device until you remove a swimmer or delete the app.

Your choices

Remove a swimmer in Settings to delete their entries from your device; delete the app to remove all local data. Cloud copies expire automatically as above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change. Questions: ben.rohrs@gmail.com.
